Training & Development

How the Sinawe Tutor Programme under the guidance of our programme coordinator, Mrs Yawa has prepared me to support students in Information Systems III, both pedagogically and professionally.

9 min read Sinawe Tutor Programme

On this page

  • Overview
  • Core Training
  • Planning & Time
  • Professional Skills
  • Support for My Journey

01 Overview

As a tutor in the Sinawe Tutor Programme, with the guidance and support of Mrs Yawa, our programme coordinator, I have received training and development aimed at preparing me to effectively support students in Information Systems III. The training has helped me develop both pedagogical and professional skills that are important for creating meaningful and productive learning experiences.

Through workshops, training sessions, and practical activities, I have gained a better understanding of my responsibilities as a tutor and the importance of being a facilitator rather than simply providing students with answers. The training has also strengthened my confidence, communication, leadership, planning, and classroom management skills. These skills are valuable not only in my tutoring role but also in my long-term goal of becoming an Applications Developer.

02 Core Training Components & Skills

The training provided me with practical techniques that I can apply during tutorial sessions. It focused on creating an environment where students feel comfortable participating, asking questions, and working through challenging concepts.

The key competencies and skills I developed include:

Student Engagement & Active Learning

I learned how to encourage students to participate actively through discussions, questions, problem-solving activities, and practical exercises rather than relying only on passive learning.

Facilitation Skills

I learned to take the role of a facilitator who guides students towards understanding instead of simply giving them solutions. This allows students to develop their own problem-solving and critical-thinking abilities.

Adapting to Different Learning Needs

The training helped me understand that students have different levels of knowledge and different ways of learning. I learned to adjust my explanations and teaching approaches according to the needs of the students.

Explaining Complex Concepts Clearly

Since Information Systems III covers areas such as IT project management, database design, advanced SQL, transaction management, and distributed databases, I learned the importance of breaking complex concepts into smaller and easier-to-understand parts.

Collaborative & Peer Learning

I developed an appreciation for peer-to-peer learning and the value of students learning from one another. Group discussions and collaborative activities can help students gain different perspectives and become more confident in sharing their ideas.

Supportive Learning Environment

The training emphasised the importance of creating an environment where students feel respected and comfortable asking questions. I learned that a supportive environment can encourage greater participation and improve students' confidence.

03 Planning & Time Management

Effective tutoring requires preparation and proper management of time. Through the training, I learned how important it is to prepare before each tutorial session and ensure that the available time is used effectively.

01

Session Planning

I learned how to structure a tutorial session by identifying the topic, preparing relevant activities, considering students' existing knowledge, and setting clear objectives for the session.

02

Content Prioritisation

I learned how to identify the most important concepts that students need to understand and focus on areas that are likely to require additional explanation or practice.

03

Time Management

I developed strategies for allocating appropriate time to different activities during a tutorial. This helps ensure that important concepts are covered while still allowing students enough time to ask questions and practise what they have learned.

04

Preparation & Organisation

The training encouraged me to remain organised by preparing tutorial materials and activities in advance and ensuring that my sessions support the objectives of the module.

04 Critical Skills for Professional Development

The training has also contributed significantly to my professional development and supports my career goal of becoming an Applications Developer.

Problem-Solving Skills

Tutoring requires me to understand students' challenges and find appropriate ways to help them overcome difficulties. This has strengthened my ability to analyse problems and approach them from different perspectives.

Communication Skills

Regular interaction with students has helped me improve my ability to communicate technical information clearly and effectively. I have also learned the importance of listening carefully to students before responding to their questions.

Leadership & Responsibility

Through my role as a tutor, I have developed greater leadership skills. I have learned to take responsibility for my preparation, the quality of my tutorial sessions, and the support I provide to students.

Patience & Empathy

The training helped me understand that students progress at different rates. I have therefore learned to be patient and empathetic when assisting students who may struggle with certain concepts.

Adaptability & Flexibility

I learned that not every tutorial session will go according to plan. Being able to adjust my approach based on students' questions, participation, and level of understanding is an important part of effective tutoring.

05 Support for My Tutoring Journey

The training and guidance I received from the Sinawe Tutor Programme, particularly with the support of Mrs Yawa, have helped prepare me for my tutoring responsibilities in Information Systems III.

Maintaining Quality & Alignment

I learned the importance of ensuring that my tutorial activities are aligned with the module content, learning outcomes, and guidance provided by the module lecturer. This helps ensure that students receive consistent and relevant academic support.

Building Confidence & Accountability

The training has increased my confidence in working with students while also teaching me to take responsibility for my role as a tutor. I have learned the importance of being prepared, reliable, and professional.

Reflecting & Improving

I learned that effective tutoring requires continuous reflection. After each session, I can consider what worked well, identify areas that could be improved, and use students' feedback to improve future sessions.

Supporting Student Success

The training has taught me that my role is not only to explain academic content but also to encourage students to become independent learners. My aim is to help students develop the confidence and skills to solve problems on their own.

Career Development

The communication, leadership, problem-solving, teamwork, planning, and adaptability skills I have gained through the programme are transferable to the workplace. These skills will contribute to my professional development and support my long-term goal of becoming an Applications Developer.

Overall, the training and development I have received have helped me become more confident, organised, and effective as a tutor. With the guidance of Mrs Yawa and the support provided through the programme, I am better prepared to create meaningful learning experiences and contribute positively to the academic development of the students I support.
