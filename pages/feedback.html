04 What Students Valued

Written comments highlighted several recurring themes. I have grouped them below with representative quotes drawn from the questionnaire.

Patience & Willingness to Help

Students appreciated being able to ask again and again until they understood.

"You were always patient with us making sure we get what we saying."

Clear Explanations

Students valued having difficult concepts unpacked into simpler terms.

"The way you break down complex concepts and make them easier to understand."

Approachability & Dedication

Respondents described me as friendly, patient, supportive and willing to devote time to help.

"He even dedicated his whole day helping us."

Assessment Preparation

Several students reported the support helped them prepare for their tests and presentations.

"The overall support was best, it helped me a lot on my tests, especially TP."

05 Areas for Improvement

Positive feedback is encouraging, but constructive comments are especially valuable for showing professional growth. Here is what students identified.

01

Speaking volume in face-to-face sessions

Some students suggested raising my voice during contact classes.

02

Session timing & delivery mode

Students recommended morning classes to improve attendance, or online sessions where appropriate.

03

Pace of step-by-step explanations

Allow more time so everyone can follow along and stay on the same page.

04

More quizzes & practice

Some students requested additional quiz activities and practical exercises.

05

Confidence during contact sessions

One student encouraged me not to panic or feel intimidated when teaching in person.

07 Reflection

This feedback helped me recognise the importance of patience, clear communication, and adapting explanations to students' learning needs. It also reminded me that effective tutoring involves more than explaining content, it requires creating an environment where students feel comfortable asking questions.

In future sessions, I intend to improve my confidence when presenting, allow more time for step-by-step explanations, and include additional quizzes and practical activities. I will also consider student feedback when planning session times and delivery methods.

Overall, this experience has helped me identify my strengths and areas for continued development as a tutor. I am grateful to the students who took the time to complete the questionnaire honestly, their feedback shapes how I approach every session that follows.
